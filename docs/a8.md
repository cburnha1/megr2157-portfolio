# A8 – Gears

## Objective
The assignments objective was to design a gearbox which can be assembled and disassembled. The spur gears have a gear ratio of 24:9. The output gear torque and speed are to be 1.25 in-lbf and 1100 rpm relative. The input (pinion,power-transmission) and output (gear, short shaft) shaft diameters are .125 and .25 inches respectively and each have a length of 1 inch.

## Analyze
I started by doing all of the math necessary for the task. I listed everything that was known and what I needed to find. I checked to see if the shafts would be strong enough for the project. I then solved for the journal bearings and made a rough sketch of the gearbox shape.
