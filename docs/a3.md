## Communicate

### Engineering Lessons Learned
* **Boundary Condition Stiffening in FEA:** Restraining all degrees of freedom on a 3D face prevents natural Poisson contraction, creating artificial localized stress and stiffness artifacts near fixed supports compared to 1D theory.
* **Impact of Geometric Discontinuities ($K_t$):** Small features like pin holes dramatically amplify local stress ($K_t \approx 2.0\text{--}3.0$), demonstrating why nominal stresses alone cannot guarantee structural integrity near fasteners.
* **Verification of Hand vs. FEA:** Simple geometries should always be verified against parametric hand calculations before accepting FEA output to catch setup, unit, or meshing errors.
