## 7. Floating Bearing Mount

### Overview
The floating bearing mount supports the lower end of the **0.2500 in** lead screw while allowing free rotation and axial float to accommodate thermal expansion and frame deflection without binding. The mount is designed to fasten directly to the bottom of the heavy 40-series T-slot frame.

----

### 7a. Size and Location Justifications

* **Base Footprint (`1.50 in × 1.50 in`):** Sized to sit flush against the 40-series T-slot extrusion profile ($40\text{ mm} \approx 1.57\text{ in}$), providing a stable mounting interface without overhanging the frame edges while leaving adequate edge distance for the mounting fasteners.
* **Base Thickness (`0.25 in`):** Provides sufficient bearing strength and stiffness beneath the fastener heads to resist clamping loads and minor radial/transverse reactions from the lead screw without localized yielding or bending.
* **Central Bore Diameter (`Ø 0.2500 in`):** Sized to accept the `0.25 in` lead screw journal with an **ANSI B4.1 RC3 (Precision Running Fit)**, preventing radial wobble/whipping while permitting smooth rotation and axial translation.
* **Central Bore Location (`0.750 in, 0.750 in`):** Centered on the base relative to the datum edges so the lower bearing axis remains concentric with the upper motor shaft and coupler axis.
* **Mounting Fastener Clearance Holes (`#10 Screws` — `Ø 0.221 in`, #2 Drill):** Selected per *Machinery’s Handbook* / ASME B18.2.8 for a **normal fit** clearance hole on a #10 fastener ($0.1900\text{ in}$ nominal major diameter), ensuring clean bolt passage and minor positional adjustment during alignment.
* **Mounting Hole Pattern (`0.25 in` from edges / `1.000 in` center-to-center spacing):** Positioned symmetrically around the central boss to clear the #10 socket head cap screw heads ($\sim 0.312\text{ in}$ head diameter), maintain safe edge distance, and align cleanly with the T-slot channels.

---

### 7b. Functional Tolerances (ASME Y14.5 / Course Titleblock)

| Feature | Nominal Dimension | Tolerance | Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Bore Diameter (RC3 Hole, H7)** | `Ø 0.2500 in` | `+0.0006 / -0.0000 in` | Overrides standard titleblock tolerance to achieve an **ANSI B4.1 RC3 precision running fit** (`0.0005–0.0015 in` diametral clearance when paired with an `f6` shaft journal of `Ø 0.2500 -0.0005/-0.0009 in`). |
| **Bore Center Location** | `0.750 in` (X & Y) | `± 0.005 in` | Three-place decimal tolerance maintains concentricity between the upper drive train and lower floating mount to prevent lead screw binding. |
| **Fastener Hole Spacing** | `1.000 in` | `± 0.005 in` | Three-place decimal tolerance ensures hole-to-hole alignment with the T-slot nut pattern without side-loading the fasteners. |
| **#10 Clearance Hole Diameter** | `Ø 0.221 in` | `± 0.005 in` | Standard three-place decimal tolerance per course titleblock for drilled clearance holes. |
| **Base Outer Width & Length** | `1.50 in` | `± 0.01 in` | Two-place decimal tolerance; outer envelope dimensions are non-critical to shaft alignment. |
| **Base Plate Thickness** | `0.25 in` | `± 0.01 in` | Two-place decimal tolerance; sufficient to maintain clamping stiffness and boss height accuracy. |
