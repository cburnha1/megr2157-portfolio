## Analyze
Caleb Burnham - Floating Bearing Mount

I designed the floating bearing mount to support the lower end of the **0.2500 in** lead screw while allowing it to rotate and float axially without binding.

* **Base Footprint (`1.50 in × 1.50 in`):** Sized to sit flush against the 40-series T-slot frame without overhanging the edges while leaving enough room for the mounting bolts.
* **Base Thickness (`0.25 in`):** Thick enough to support the clamping load from the bolts and resist bending from any side loads on the lead screw.
* **Central Bore (`Ø 0.2500 in`, `+0.0006 / -0.0000 in`):** Sized using an ANSI B4.1 **RC3 precision running fit** (H7 hole / f6 shaft) so the lead screw spins freely without radial wobble or binding.
* **Bore Location (`0.750 in, 0.750 in`):** Centered on the base (`± 0.005 in`) to stay aligned with the upper motor shaft and coupler axis.
* **Mounting Holes (`#10 Screws` — `Ø 0.221 in`, #2 Drill):** Selected from the *Machinery’s Handbook* for a normal fit clearance hole (`± 0.005 in`), spaced `1.000 in` apart (`0.25 in` from each edge) to clear the screw heads and line up with the T-slots.
* **Outer Base Tolerances (`± 0.01 in`):** Standard two-place decimal tolerances applied to the `1.50 in` outer width/length and `0.25 in` thickness since they do not affect shaft alignment.

## Decide
An RC3 running fit and #10 normal-fit clearance holes were chosen so the mount holds the lead screw concentric with the upper drivetrain while still allowing easy assembly and axial float during operation.
