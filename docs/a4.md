# A4 – Motor Mount

## Objective
Design a motor mount around both stress and strain constraints to withstand a 300 N force at the end of the shaft of the motor, while maintaining a safety factor of 3.

## Analyze
First I based everything around the motor that was provided. All the dimensions are in mm.

![motorsketch](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d5ce9de0-0ea4-4267-8e67-fa45d47364bb)

I researched the material options provided for 100% infill PLA:
* **Yield Strength ($\sigma_y$):** 40 MPa
* **Elastic Modulus ($E$):** 2600 MPa
* **Factor of Safety ($n$):** 3
* **Allowable Stress ($\sigma_{\text{allow}}$):** 13.33 MPa

## Decide
I started by sketching the rough shape that I wanted the mount to be. I then used this to determine the height and width. With both of these values solved I will be able to solve for the thickness that each member needs to be in order to meet the design constraints.

![MotorISo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03e2e51c-5905-49d6-ba74-37fc1222ca23)

**Feature 1:**

I started by calculating the thickness needed to meet the stress requirements and deflection requirements.

* **Knowns:** Load $P = 300\text{ N}$, $\sigma_y = 40\text{ MPa}$, $E = 2600\text{ MPa}$, $n = 3$, $\sigma_{\text{allow}} = 13.33\text{ MPa}$, Base Width $h = 50\text{ mm}$, Length $L = 92.6\text{ mm}$, Max Deflection $\delta_{\text{max}} = 0.30\text{ mm}$.
* **Unknowns:** Bending Stress Thickness ($t_{\text{stress}}$), Deflection Thickness ($t_{\text{def}}$).

![motorf1](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fc237114-c632-4325-aa88-bcc99f29c5bc)

I went through the beam calculations that we covered in class and plugged in values that were provided for both the stress and deflection calculations. Deflection governed for Feature 1, giving a final thickness of **29.02 mm**.

---

**Feature 2:**

Then I calculated the thickness using the same process I used for Feature 1.

* **Knowns:** Load $P = 300\text{ N}$, $n = 3$, $\sigma = 40\text{ MPa}$, $E = 2600\text{ MPa}$, Height $h = 50\text{ mm}$, Base Length $L = 92.6\text{ mm}$.
* **Unknowns:** Stress Thickness ($t_{\text{stress}}$).

![motorf2](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/be843d09-a46f-4e22-aa59-0b931a975aed)

Solving for stress yields a required thickness of **15.81 mm** for Feature 2.
