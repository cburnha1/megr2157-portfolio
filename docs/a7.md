## Communicate & Engineering Reflections
### Engineering Lessons Learned
Throughout this FEA study, I observed that refining the mesh size directly increases computational solve times while providing more accurate stress concentrations near geometric discontinuities and sharp fillets. Coarser meshes underestimated peak stress values due to averaging across larger elements, whereas converged fine meshes accurately captured localized stress risers. By iteratively removing material from low-stress regions identified in the outer loop, the final bracket design successfully achieved a lower total mass while maintaining a safe structural factor of safety.
